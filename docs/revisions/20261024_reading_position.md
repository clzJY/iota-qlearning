# Named report parts and reading position

The five required parts use the assignment titles as their main headings. The
comparison is a section at the same level as results; its additional investigations
remain marked as further work. References and AI use belong with Part 5 takeaways.
Supporting reproduction files follow the five report parts.

The detailed cards have been replaced by one row of five native quick links at
the bottom of The Problem. The row scrolls horizontally on narrow screens. A
sticky indicator shows the current part and experiment while scrolling, highlights
the relevant numbered link, and updates for deep links, resizing, and opened
optional evidence. Native quick links and all report content remain available
without JavaScript; the sticky enhancement is omitted from the printable PDF.

The experiments and saved numerical results are unchanged from the corrected-map
release. Browser validation checks all five named sections, ten reading locations,
quick-link navigation, and a single quick-link row at mobile/tablet/desktop sizes.
